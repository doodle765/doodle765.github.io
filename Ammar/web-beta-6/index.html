Before you start

GuideLens is an experimental assistive aid. It helps you notice obstacles, read signs and follow walking routes — but it cannot replace your white cane, guide dog, or your own judgment.

Always cross roads the way you were trained. The app will never tell you a road is "safe to cross" — it only reports what it sees.

Camera video never leaves your phone. Location is used only when you ask.
